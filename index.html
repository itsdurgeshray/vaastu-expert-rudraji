What happens during a Vastu consultation?

Panditji studies the directions, entrance, rooms and placement of things in your space. He explains what is working well, what needs attention, and gives simple remedies that suit your home and budget.

Will I need to break walls or rebuild?

In most cases, no. Panditji focuses on practical remedies such as changing the placement of furniture, colours, and simple Vastu corrections that need no demolition.

How do I pay?

No payment is needed to send a request. Once Panditji confirms your time on WhatsApp, pay the dakshina with any UPI app such as Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm or BHIM, and share the screenshot in the same chat.

Can I consult from another city or abroad?

Yes. Choose the WhatsApp video call option while booking. Keep your floor plan or a simple hand drawn sketch ready, and Panditji will guide you room by room.

What should I keep ready?

A floor plan or simple sketch of your home or shop, the direction of your main door if you know it, a few photos of each room, and the concerns you would like to discuss.
